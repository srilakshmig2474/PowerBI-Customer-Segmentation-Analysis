# 📊 Power BI Customer Segmentation & Spending Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer demographics, spending behavior, purchasing patterns, customer segments, and campaign response.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Customer Segmentation & Spending Analysis Dashboard** transforms customer data into meaningful business insights using interactive Power BI visualizations.

The project consists of three analytical dashboards:

1. **Customer Demographics Overview**
2. **Customer Spending & Purchase Behavior**
3. **Customer Segmentation & Business Insights**

The dashboard allows users to explore customer characteristics, spending patterns, purchasing behavior, customer value segments, and campaign performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics
- Understand customer income levels
- Study education and age-group distribution
- Analyze spending and purchasing behavior
- Identify high, medium, and low-value customers
- Compare customer income with purchasing behavior
- Analyze campaign acceptance
- Support data-driven customer marketing decisions

# 💡 Top 10 Key Insights

1. The dataset contains approximately **2.216K customers**, providing a broad customer base for segmentation analysis.

2. The customer base contains different **income levels**, with medium-income customers representing the largest income group.

3. **Graduation is the most common education level**, representing approximately half of the customers.

4. The **46–60 age group** contains the largest number of customers, followed by the 60+ and 31–45 groups.

5. **Marital status is associated with differences in average income**, with some customer groups showing noticeably higher average income than others.

6. Customers generated approximately **₹1M in total spending**, with an average spending of around **₹607 per customer**.

7. **Store purchases are higher than web purchases**, indicating stronger customer activity through physical-store purchasing.

8. **Low-value customers form the largest customer segment**, followed by medium-value and high-value customers.

9. **High-value customers show stronger spending and purchasing behavior**, making them an important segment for targeted marketing strategies.

10. **Campaign acceptance varies across customer segments**, providing an opportunity to design more targeted campaigns for different customer groups.
